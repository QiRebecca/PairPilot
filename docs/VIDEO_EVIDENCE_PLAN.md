## Recording rules

- Record in English or add accurate English subtitles.
- Keep final public video at or below four minutes.
- If speeding playback, label the factor and preserve the complete run.
- Do not combine unrelated run IDs or present seeded facts as generated output.
- Do not show billing pages, account email, project owner identity, tokens,
  private profiles, terminal environment, or browser credential state.
- Verify the YouTube/Vimeo URL in a signed-out window before submitting.
